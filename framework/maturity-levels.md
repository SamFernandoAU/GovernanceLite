# Maturity levels and scoring

Every practice has the same three levels. They're cumulative: to be at Level 2, you also need everything in Level 1.

| Level | Name | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | **Not yet** | Some Level 1 items are missing |
| 1 | **Baseline** | All Level 1 items are done. The common incidents are covered. |
| 2 | **Established** | Level 1 plus all Level 2 items. It runs as a habit, automated where it can be, on a schedule. |
| 3 | **Evidenced** | Level 2 plus all Level 3 items. Records exist that an auditor or a customer could rely on. |

## Scoring

- Score each practice separately: its level is **the highest level where every item at that level and below is done**.
- Be honest. "Mostly" isn't done. Partly-done items become actions in the [monthly review](practices/08-monthly-review.md).
- Your **profile** is the eight scores side by side, for example `GL1:2 GL2:1 GL3:1 GL4:2 GL5:0 GL6:1 GL7:1 GL8:1`. Don't average them; the weakest practice is the one attackers find.
- Use the interactive [self-check](../#/self-check). It reads the checklists straight from the practice pages and exports Markdown for your minutes.

## Choosing a target profile

| Your situation | Suggested target |
| --- | --- |
| Early-stage startup, small team | Level 1 everywhere; Level 2 for GL2 (identity) and GL4 (pipeline) |
| Scaling company, enterprise customers asking security questions | Level 2 everywhere |
| Holding customer personal data at scale, or regulated | Level 2 everywhere; Level 3 for GL2, GL6 and GL7 |
| Preparing for ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2 | Level 3 everywhere, then the [last mile](mappings.md#whats-left-for-certification) |
| MSP managing client environments | Level 2 internally, and Level 1 as the minimum for every client |

## Re-scoring cadence

Re-score every quarter in the monthly review. Raise **one practice at a time**; trying to raise all eight at once is how frameworks die.
